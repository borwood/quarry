Write narrative for a future reader: the problem as encountered, the wrong turns, the mechanism discovered, why it was solved this way — never a changelog. One story per entry. Entries live at journal/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>.md, registered as doc nodes (kind=journal) with about edges to the areas the story touches. Write on story, never on schedule. Mark moments worth retelling publicly with a '> blogworthy:' line naming the angle. Claims born in conversation may cite the entry as source after the fact.
