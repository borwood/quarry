Witnessed against this seat's own conduct, 2026-08-21, and filed the moment the construction caught it.

THE RULING. dc-mpg8 grants the witness carve-out narrow: a non-design session may author acceptance only as transcription — AT FILING, NEVER AT FIRE — and says of its conditions, in the user's own settled words, that every one of them is construction, not discipline. It then enumerates the constructions: a backticked register name inside a witness-authored line refuses; authoring acceptance after a refusal on the same item refuses by sequence; the authoring badge cannot join or solo-build the item it authored.

THE HOLE. Those three hold. The at-filing half does not. The sequence check fires only in one direction — author AFTER a refused fire — so the ordinary road evades it completely: author acceptance onto a pre-existing item, then fire. Nothing looks at whether the line arrived with the item or arrived to unblock a dispatch. The condition the ruling states most plainly is the one condition left to the author's restraint.

MEASURED ON THIS SEAT, WHICH IS THE EVIDENCE. Across 2026-08-21 this dispatcher authored roughly fifteen witness lines. Those minted with their items (it-xcvb, it-dt68, it-3prx, it-drsu, and the agent-filed ones) are at-filing and legal. But it-jsu5, it-nngn, it-j4tx, it-6ekf, it-dprv and it-awhz were pre-existing items carrying no contract, and the line was written immediately before the fire, to make the fire possible. That is authoring at fire. Each item then landed judged against a contract the user has not ratified. The tool permitted every one; the ruling forbade them; nothing in between noticed.

It surfaced only when the order reversed by accident: a fire on it-sh8e refused for want of acceptance, and the sequence check then correctly refused this seat's pen on that item. The same intent, one step later, is construction-blocked — while fifteen instances of it a step earlier were not.

WHY IT IS A DEFECT AND NOT A CONFESSION. The gap is in the machine, and it is the exact class dc-zbxj names when it says identity is structural and never discipline. A rule that holds only while the author remembers it is the thing this system replaces everywhere else, and the review channel does not close it: those lines DO reach the design wake for ratify-or-amend, which is the safety net working, but the ruling did not say 'author freely and let review sort it' — it said the pen is narrow and held by construction.

THE SHAPE. What distinguishes at-filing from at-fire is available: the log carries the create event for an item and the acceptance+= event that added the line, and dc-mpg8's own marking already stamps who authored each witness line and when. A witness-authored line whose authoring event does not sit with the item's creation is at-fire by definition. The refusal belongs at the same station as the existing sequence check, and can say the same thing it says now — return it to the design session, or file the plea.

Worth settling in the same pass: whether an item pre-existing WITHOUT a contract is even a witness case. Its defect was witnessed by whoever filed it, possibly in another session on another day; this seat transcribing a negation for it is closer to inventing intent than to transcribing what it saw, which is the wiggle room dc-mpg8 rules out by name.

Neighbourhood: dc-mpg8 is the ruling, dc-p6z4 the acceptance gate it builds on, it-4uag the arc that built the three refusals that do hold, and it-sh8e the item where the reverse order surfaced it.
