Build shape ratified in the intent ruling (intent rides items and docs; capability names are the join) and the builds-on ruling (the matrix grows builds-on) — one design cluster: intent rides existing structure, the naming register is the join, lineage gets its own rel. (1) `builds-on` enters the edge matrix: builder → built-upon, shapes decision→decision, doc→claim, doc→decision; other shapes refused with a C4-style teaching error naming the matrix; stamps at write like any edge; NO status coupling — refuting or superseding a target never flips the builder; behind reports builds-on refs at ordinary severity; blast traverses reverse builds-on so a killed spine enumerates the specs and decisions standing on it. (2) The intent delta: acceptance lines name intended capabilities in the spine register (lands `name`: what it provides); a derived query compares backtick-named acceptance lines of live items against spine claim titles sharing areas — intended-but-unlanded and landed-but-unintended, both directions, never a sweep; query name assistant-grade, advertised where the decision fires (shaping output and/or area open). (3) Teach surfaces: the skill's edge matrix line and the guide grow the rel and the query; q init --claude regenerates. (4) Retroactive lineage once the verb accepts the rel: dc-cc76 builds-on dc-grrb, dc-kpqg builds-on dc-cc76 — agent's if convenient, else dispatcher homework at harvest. Doc kinds prd/spec need no engine work (the field registry carries kinds); the engine work is the rel and the query.
