Named from the agent seat at the it-xwpw landing, 2026-08-21, and deliberately not built there — it is a feature, and the arc was a defect repair.

After it-xwpw, q join holds both answers at once for the first time: the model the dispatcher stamped with --model, and the model the harness's own record says the joining agent is actually running (cl-644e, reachable from a verb now that the chat transcript is located from the injected chat id). Nothing compares them. A dispatcher who stamps one model and spawns the agent on another gets no word of it from either seat — the stamp simply wins, silently, which is the original it-xcvb defect wearing the override's clothes.

The comparison is one call away. What makes it a feature rather than a one-liner is that a naive compare fires falsely: in the window before an arc's first turn reaches disk, the record road answers from the sidecar, which carries the dispatcher's ALIAS as typed (opus) while the stamp carries a resolved id (claude-opus-5) — cl-jp4q measured that alias as ambiguous across time besides, so no table reconciles them. Comparing those two spellings would cry mismatch on every correctly-stamped arc that joins fast.

Doing it honestly wants coord::agent_model to report WHICH road answered — resolved transcript entry or coarse sidecar alias — and the compare to speak only on the resolved road. The agent that named this estimated roughly six lines. Both halves already exist; only the source reporting is missing.

Note the check earns its keep beyond catching a typo: it is the one place that could tell a dispatcher their --model override disagrees with reality, and after cl-jp4q the override exists precisely for cases the record cannot see. A disagreement is therefore sometimes CORRECT and must confront rather than verdict — the posture framings::user_owned_reconcile already takes.

Neighbourhood: cl-644e for the two answers now in one hand, cl-jp4q for why the alias cannot be compared, cl-dqt4 for the stamp, and th-e5ez for the user's standing fork on whether --model should be demanded at all.
