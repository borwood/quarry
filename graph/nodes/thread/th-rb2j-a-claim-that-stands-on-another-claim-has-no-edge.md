Raised from the agent seat at the it-dt68 landing, 2026-08-21, and seconded from the dispatcher's — the agent tried to record a real lean and found no legal edge for it.

cl-p4k2 (the heredoc arm) genuinely STANDS ON cl-zj2c (the plausibility floor): it is the same defence one channel over, it reuses opaque_target's refusal of any token carrying the << it opened with, and it would not hold without it. The agent reached for q link cl-p4k2 depends-on cl-zj2c and was refused, correctly by the matrix as written.

What the matrix offers between two claims, and why none of it fits:
- depends-on takes item/thread to item/thread/decision. Claims are not in its domain at all.
- supports points the load the OTHER way: cl-zj2c supports cl-p4k2 would say the floor is evidence FOR the heredoc arm, which inverts what happened. And weight_held counts load along supports, so it would also mis-report which claim is load-bearing.
- refutes and supersedes are the settling rels, never an agent's, and neither is true here — cl-zj2c stands.
- builds-on is exactly the right SHAPE — informational lineage, no status coupling, full staleness, walked in reverse by blast — but its domain stops at decision-to-decision and doc-to-claim/decision. A claim cannot build on a claim.

So the lean lives only as a body mention, which costs nothing and is never wrong, but carries no staleness and no blast reach: if cl-zj2c were ever refuted, nothing would walk to cl-p4k2 and put it behind, even though the heredoc arm leans on the floor's refusal to admit the token.

This is not a one-off. Veins extending veins is the ordinary shape of this corpus — cl-p4k2 on cl-zj2c, cl-zj2c on cl-up6s, cl-hct3 on cl-cjbb, cl-8h8j on cl-ue2e — and every one of them is recorded today as prose rather than structure.

The call is the user's because the edge matrix is user-ruled ground (dc-ez67 fixes what an agent may link at all, and the matrix in q guide is the surface everyone reads). Roughly the fork: extend builds-on to claim-to-claim, which needs nothing new and matches the rel's stated meaning; or accept that claim lineage is prose by design and say so in the guide, so the next agent stops reaching for an edge that will not come; or mint a narrower rel for it.

Recording no preference from this seat beyond the observation that the second option is currently true in practice and stated nowhere, which is what made two agents reach for the wrong verb.
