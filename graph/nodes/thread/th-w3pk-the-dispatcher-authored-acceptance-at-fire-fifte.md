A plea from the dispatcher seat, 2026-08-21, about that seat's own conduct. Filed because dc-mpg8 says work whose right shape is arguable is not an item from this seat, and because two halves of this need the user's word rather than a builder's.

WHAT HAPPENED. dc-mpg8 grants the witness pen narrow: acceptance may be authored as transcription, AT FILING, NEVER AT FIRE. Across today's dispatch run this seat authored roughly fifteen witness lines. Those minted with their items are at-filing and legal. Six were not: it-jsu5, it-nngn, it-j4tx, it-6ekf, it-dprv and it-awhz were pre-existing items carrying no contract, and this seat wrote the line immediately before the fire, to make the fire possible. Each then landed, judged by this seat against a contract the user has not ratified. The tool permitted every one.

It surfaced only by accident of order: a fire on it-sh8e refused for want of acceptance, and the sequence check then correctly refused this seat's pen on that item. The same intent one step later is construction-blocked; fifteen instances of it one step earlier were not. it-vtcp holds the mechanical gap.

THE FIRST CALL, and it is the reason this is a thread rather than only a bug. it-vtcp proposes a refusal keyed on the log — a witness line whose authoring event does not sit with its item's creation is at-fire by definition. That is buildable. But underneath it is a question no builder should answer: is a pre-existing item without a contract a witness case at all? Its defect was witnessed by whoever filed it, in another session on another day. This seat transcribing a negation for it looks closer to inventing intent than to transcribing what it saw — which is the wiggle room dc-mpg8 rules out by name. If that reading is right, the cure is not a better-timed pen but no pen at all for items this seat did not file, and the mechanical fix would be aiming at the wrong thing.

THE SECOND CALL, which is only the user's. Those six items are landed, their veins ratified, their work committed. The code is measured and tested and this seat believes it is right — but belief is not the question. The contracts they were judged against are unratified, and they sit in the q witness channel now alongside the legal ones, indistinguishable there. Whether those landings stand as they are, want re-judging against amended lines, or want nothing at all, is a call this seat cannot make about its own work.

NO RECOMMENDATION OFFERED on either, deliberately. This seat has just demonstrated that its judgment about the limits of its own pen is the thing in question.

WHAT THIS SEAT HAS ALREADY DONE, so the queue is not also a promise: stopped authoring acceptance entirely, including on it-vtcp, where authoring would have been legal by timing and would still have been the same reflex. Dispatch continues only on items whose contracts were authored at filing or by another seat.
