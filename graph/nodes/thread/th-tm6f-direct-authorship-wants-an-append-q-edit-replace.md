Surfaced building it-gwj7. The spend-down prompt (framings::SPEND_DOWN, dc-hzrm) teaches direct authorship - append the state paragraph, the evidence note, the dogear - but q edit only replaces a body whole (--body/--body-file), so the taught move is read, hand-compose the full body plus the new paragraph, replace. The prompt words around the gap (q edit <id> --body-file <the full body plus your paragraph>). An --append flag would make the boundary vehicle one act instead of three and remove the replace-risk of losing paragraphs to a mis-paste. Open: is append its own verb, a flag on edit, or is the compose-replace friction acceptable at the boundary?
