House rules, charters, blogworthy flags, lens taxonomies, extra fields, procedure text — all per-project protocol, committed in the host repo's graph/, never quarry features. The tool ships the invariant machinery (types, edges, versioning, constraints, homework); each project ships its protocol (verb-riding instructions, declared fields). Subtraction test: subtract the project and quarry must remain featureless and true. Hardcoding a charter into the engine is the DeepAxis mistake.
