Archived is a flag, never a file move: ids resolve, edges hold, blast and behind always see everything — correctness surfaces never filter. Archive by status, never by age (age may prompt, wrap suggests). Parents with live children refuse to archive — a parent indexes its archived offspring, so aging leaves are found through what they were part of. Docs and journals never archive; areas retire by status; superseded decisions are routine candidates. No surface hides silently: every hiding surface reports a count of archived/hidden and the way to reach them (CLI --all, view toggles merging archived into natural sort, find labels). The view collapses long settled lists past a protocol-configurable count. Archiving does not bump v. Session retirement removes the registry entry, releases leases, and logs last rites; at the wrap of an EPHEMERAL session, retiring is the agent's default close act, announced — with conversion to durable offered instead when the purview proved lasting.
