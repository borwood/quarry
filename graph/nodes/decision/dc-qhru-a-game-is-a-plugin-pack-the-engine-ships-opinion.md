The engine ships with plugins that build on engine modules, representing opinions on solving common game needs — inspired by other voxel games and especially their modding communities. A game is a plugin pack that includes a plugin layer for its launcher: shipped games can carry their own launcher and menu of worlds (click to configure and generate a new world). Alternatively players use the (unbuilt) engine launcher to jump into or generate worlds from plugin packs, or launch the (unbuilt) editor. Participation in the engine launcher and (unbuilt) plugin marketplace is likely opt-in: people should own what they make. End goal: a shipped game can bear recognizable family relation to other games on the engine, or totally unrecognizable ancestry, depending on how much custom work is done.
