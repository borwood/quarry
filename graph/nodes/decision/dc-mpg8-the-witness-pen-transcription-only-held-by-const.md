Settled 2026-08-18, ruling on the dispatcher's plea. THE CARVE-OUT, GRANTED NARROW: a non-design session may author acceptance only as transcription - at filing, never at fire; the line is a witnessed defect's negation, never invented intent; the author never executes; no new register names. Every condition is construction, not discipline: a backticked register name inside a witness-authored line refuses; authoring acceptance after a refusal on the same item refuses by sequence; the authoring badge cannot join or solo-build the item it authored. Anything with creative wiggle room - work subsumable in planned systems, lines implying design intent, repairs whose right shape is arguable - is not an item from that seat: it lands as evidence on the thread it informs, or as a new thread when the session has a plea from its unique context and positioning (this thread is the model - the plea channel is wanted; fix-on-notice, dc-wcyc, never meant fix-by-self-authorization). The list of what a dispatcher may author and fire with no human in the loop is genuinely small, and the machinery keeps it small. THE FLAG ROUTING, AMENDED FROM THE PROPOSAL: witness-authored lines do not wait for review in the authoring session's own conversation - the user does not attend the dispatcher. The user attends ONE LIAISON - the design session, for the time being, until a better shape emerges - so witness-authored lines are marked at authoring and surface on the design wake's review channel until ratified or amended, the composed-strings flow (th-4w3a, dc-vzvf) being the model. The three inaugural instances (it-4p7m, it-ez2y, it-kjgz) are legal under this rule and enter that channel rather than being blanket-blessed here.
