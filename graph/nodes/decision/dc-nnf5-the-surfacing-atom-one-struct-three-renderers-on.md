Ruled 2026-08-11 by the user. One atom struct, three renderers, all owned by one surface module; no node field is formatted anywhere else, enforced by lint — front.title outside the module fails wrap or CI. The atom carries: id, title, type, kind, status, v, areas, provenance (with source/method presence for claims), archived. The renderers: atom_line — the full atom on one line, for every list register (find, queue, ready, shaping, touches, homework, brief shelves, hook enumerations); kind rides type, areas render as titles. atom_ref — the floor for every binary-composed sentence (errors, confirmations, refusals): "title" [type status] (id), status unconditional, archived riding the status slot. atom_unpack — body renders expand authored ids in place from the same struct, id-anchored, announcing areas only when the cited node's areas differ from the citing node's: foreign mentions announce themselves, home stays quiet. Registers differ only in how much of the atom they render, never in where they are built. Deliberately excluded: find match hygiene (substring hits — 'cli' inside 'click' admitted dc-qhru as debris) is a separate defect, filed as its own item.
