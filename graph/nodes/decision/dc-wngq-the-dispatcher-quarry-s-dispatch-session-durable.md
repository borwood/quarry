For this project: one dispatch-kind session, named dispatcher - the name needs no interpretation. steward retires as verbiage: an agent-coined term the user rejects. Durable, a regular session (persistence is the default; nothing special about its machinery). Purview: everything, for the time being - it can dispatch any item in the graph; the total overlap with quarry [design] is deliberate - same ground, different role, legible through the kind field (dc-ad8b). More dispatch sessions are mintable as need arises; naming stays plain. The user's phrasing "has lease covering everything" is recorded as coverage: at rest a session holds a purview (dc-qeup); leases attach per-item at its dispatches. If standing leases for dispatch sessions are ever wanted, that is a separate promotion, not implied here.
