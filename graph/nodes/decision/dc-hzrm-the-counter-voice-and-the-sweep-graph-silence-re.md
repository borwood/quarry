Settled in two calls, 2026-08-18. CALL ONE, the boundary: wrap asks the spend-down question over the session's deep-touch list (derived from per-session acts, ranked by touch depth) - prompt never gate, once per session, the boundary itself the waiter (dc-dty5). The vehicle is direct authorship: the author session appends the state paragraphs, evidence notes, and dogears itself. Readings-as-homework was argued and rejected: the next session inherits importance claims it can only take on faith - the best-positioned judge for what dying context holds is the dying context. Ripple is behind working as designed (citers affirm if untouched, absorb if not); deferral-teaching is impossible by mechanism (the session meets the designated moment only when wrap announces it); the prompt carries a short Goodhart phrase (dc-dsdm) and the enrichment attitude recorded verbatim in the register corpus (do-dspv, 2026-08-18): think stigmergically - what must the next cold session re-derive, at what cost and consequence; hold enrichments lightly, honest about scope and gaps, skeptical of the session's own perspective as one active slice of experience of the graph; write for the future cold reader (user reasoning and intent, warnings, constellation-making connections, patterns that return for a repo searching priors rather than inventing), especially where the enrichment depends on context only this session holds. CALL TWO, mid-session: the counter-voice reminder - every ambient surface today speaks the graph's frame; this is the one line that speaks for the prose channel. Fired on graph silence, never the clock: N turns since the acting session's last graph act draws one reminder line into injected context; any graph act resets the counter; a session that is filing never sees it. Dense with teaching at first encounter, a light phrase after. See-something-say-something remains the ideal; the reminder narrows the leak; the wrap sweep nets the rest. HABITUATION, the known failure mode of this mechanism class (the deepcraft warning autopsy): not watched - a watch trigger would depend on the user catching a loss, and habituation is when-not-if. Its defense queues as a build in shaping, deliberately unready, to return through the almost-ready machinery for design attention when it surfaces.
