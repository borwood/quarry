User-ruled 2026-08-16: for every term the graph or its surfaces adopt, imagine the contextless agent meeting it for the first time. The word's pre-loaded prior should do the teaching; where it cannot, the surface at the moment of encounter must over-teach. This is the operational test behind dc-dsdm's word inventory (borrow, never coin) and dc-e6pp's meme-by-use: run the encounter in imagination before the term ships.

First applications: reading passes natively (a value taken off an instrument at a moment - the prior IS the semantics); measured under-teaches (a past participle naming the grounding, not the liveness), so its choke points carry what the word cannot; the vein register itself (dc-eq9d) is the standing example of a prior doing the work - precious, prospected-for, assayed arrive pre-loaded.
