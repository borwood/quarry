Ratified 2026-08-10. Intent rides existing structure; the naming register is the join. PRDs and tech specs are doc kinds (kind=prd, kind=spec), bodies at host paths, registered whole - no intent node type: an item IS intent (acceptance[] the intended outcomes, write_set[] the intended surface), and a parallel intent layer would be a second authority that drifts. Alternatives-considered live in the doc body until something depends on one (extraction-on-citation). Forks become threads; settled direction becomes decisions; specs cite the spines they build from via builds-on (see the edge-matrix ruling), so a killed capability puts its specs behind and blast enumerates them. The genuinely new move: acceptance lines name their intended capabilities in the spine register (lands `name`: what it provides) - intent and existence share vocabulary, so their comparison is derivable, both directions: intended-but-unlanded (named in acceptance/specs, no spine carries it) and landed-but-unintended (a spine no intent named - emergent scope, visible instead of silent). Lifecycle: PRD ratified -> arc item, part-of slices -> threads for open forks -> spec per slice -> landing mints spines -> the deltas derive. Extends dc-e6pp: names are the join key between plan and reality.
