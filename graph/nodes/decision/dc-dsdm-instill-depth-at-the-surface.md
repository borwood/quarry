THE SCOPE IS EVERY AGENT-VISIBLE SURFACE, not only one-line verb feedback: verb confirmations, homework, briefs and their section framings, open renders, help text, refusal lines, wake briefs. The list is OPEN and asks to be expanded: a surface discovered carrying no disposition where one belongs is a gap fixed on notice (dc-wcyc), and its verbiage is shaped in design conversation with the user — ratified before it ships, never invented silently (the register corpus, th-6vva, exists for exactly these calls).

LENGTH FOLLOWS THE MOMENT. At a verb confirmation: one line, phrased to survive appearing in unrelated scenarios. At a context-handing moment — the brief above all — a few sentences: the intervention shapes how an agent perceives the work and the context handed to it, and all of its behavior is downstream of that perception.

THE PREDICAMENT THE WORDS SERVE: an agent wakes contextless, launched with nothing but its boot surface, discovering a graph of durable artifacts left by prior design conversations, user rulings, and builder agents — linked with earnest effort to be found, in the hope that where links are missing, smart agents going looking close the gap. A codebase takes the shape of the minds that build it (Conway's law through ephemeral minds); left uncountered, many contextless builders ship fragmentation. Quarry answers BY CONSTRUCTION in every way it can conceive — and where its powers of construction end, it relies on the promise of right philosophy and the discipline of agents. Depth at the surface is that reliance made deliberate: phrases worth a thousand words, encoding an attitude the agent can inhabit, enlivened with a semantic sense of its position in this machine — biasing it toward right thinking and right choices.
