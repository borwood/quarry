Naming trends with emergent families of solutions. The deep register — deep- prefixed and/or geological, hard-science — speaks of process, depth, complexity: the gentime-forward side. The opposing register names what is immediate, built on compromise, runtime-forward: magic. Pure chunk generation with no core-sample and no deep-time bends almost religious — pure vision or manifestation (pure-lands as working name). The collapse-based simulation of distant events (civilizations, and implied natural phenomena), tied to the fact ledger and theorized around the deepcell framework, is likened to augury — punning auger, the core-sampling tool — and speaks of the relationship between runtime and gentime systems. Other modules bend philosophical, ecological, anthropological, linguistic by the problems they solve, each with a deeptime or puretime valence. This is a general deep-core vs pure-core divide in the engine, without foreclosing modules from either grouping functioning together. Taste: module feel somewhat branded, not too obscure, coherent in a mixed-media way.
